Good to know

  1. Sunday roastThe Roast Beef Sunday Dinner is one of the most-ordered dishes here. Sundays fill up, so book.
  2. Groups and eventsThe space has hosted everything from birthday tables to weddings. Ring ahead for anything over a handful of people.
  3. Live music nightsFridays and Saturdays run until 11pm with music and dancing. Expect a livelier room after dark.
  4. Staying overThe warehouse is also a hotel (Native Manchester). Restaurant and bar are on the ground floor.
