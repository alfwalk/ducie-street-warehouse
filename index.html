The space

Six floors of iron, brick and light.

A glass-roofed atrium, a long bar down the ground floor, sofas to sink into and a terrace for when Manchester behaves.

About

One warehouse,
all day long.

Ducie Street Warehouse is a bar, restaurant and hotel in one warehouse-chic space a few minutes from Piccadilly. Mornings are for coffee, breakfast and a quiet corner. By evening the lights come down, the music comes up and the bar takes over.

The kitchen keeps it laid-back: small plates, mac and cheese, a Sunday roast people keep coming back for, alongside a cocktail list and a well-stocked beer selection. Live music and dancing round out the weekend.
