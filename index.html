About the Warehouse

One warehouse.
All day long.

Ducie Street Warehouse is a bar, restaurant and hotel in one warehouse-chic space a few minutes from Piccadilly. Mornings are for coffee, breakfast and a quiet corner. By evening the lights come down, the music comes up and the bar takes over.

The kitchen keeps it laid-back — small plates, mac and cheese, a Sunday roast that people keep coming back for — alongside a cocktail list and a well-stocked beer selection. Live music and dancing round out the weekend.
